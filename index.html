Welcome to Wake Forest, North Carolina!

Welcome to Wake Forest, North Carolina! Wake Forest is a beautiful and fun town to visit, there are many things for first time visitors, families and, foodies to discover here.In this local guide you will find places to eat, grab a drink, things to do and, places to see. Wake Forest was founded in 1880. This town may have started small but has grown into a bustling and vibrant community. Wake Forest is unique because there is so much this town has to offer.
